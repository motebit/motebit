Federation signed messages v2 (`spec/relay-federation-v1.md` §3.5): `signFederationMessage` / `verifyFederationMessage`, the one producer and one verifier of relay-to-relay signatures that are domain-separated by role (`kind`), signer (`sender`), intended verifier (`recipient`) and freshness (`ts` + single-use `nonce`), committing to the body by `body_sha256`. Signed bytes are `"motebit-fed/v2\0" ‖ kind ‖ "\0" ‖ JCS(envelope)` under `motebit-jcs-ed25519-hex-v1`. Kinds today: `heartbeat`, `heartbeat-ack`, `peer-remove`. Verification is pure and fail-closed with a named refusal (`malformed`, `unknown_kind`, `kind_mismatch`, `unsupported_suite`, `wrong_recipient`, `wrong_sender`, `stale`, `body_mismatch`, `bad_signature`); the caller records the nonce in the same transaction as the write the message authorizes. The signer canonicalizes through `canonicalJsonForSigning` (an unpaired surrogate in the body or envelope throws `UnpairedSurrogateError`, nothing signed); `federationMessageBytes` takes an optional canonicalizer (default `canonicalJson`, the verifier's). Additive — no existing export changes.
