`motebit federation peer`, `mesh` and `peer-remove` speak relay-federation@1.5. A relay's handshake confirm is now minted by its own operator (self-propose is refused by 1.5 relays), so `peer` mints our confirm with our token and, when `MOTEBIT_PEER_API_TOKEN` holds the peer's master token, the peer's too; without it the peer holds us active and its operator runs `motebit federation peer <our-url>` for the other half. `mesh` takes one token per URL from `MOTEBIT_MESH_TOKENS` (comma-separated, in order), else the configured relay token for all. `peer-remove` reads the peer's relay id and asks our relay for a v2 `peer-remove` message bound to that one peer. Needs relays on relay-federation@1.5 (sibling changeset `relay-federation-v2-heartbeat-remove-ignored.md`). Each half now verifies the counterparty's v2 `peer-challenge` (from its key, to us, answering our own nonce, for our own endpoint) before asking our relay to mint anything, and the mint names the counterparty as recipient and our own endpoint — never a bare nonce — so a counterparty that hands back another relay's nonce cannot carry our confirm there. Failure steps are named `<propose|challenge|operator-confirm|confirm>-<a-to-b|b-to-a>`.
