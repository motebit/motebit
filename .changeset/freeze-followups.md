Freeze follow-ups and a subscription double credit. The proxy-token mint no longer answers a freeze-refused welcome credit as a silent zero balance: the token body states `free_credit: { status: "deferred", reason: "EMERGENCY_FROZEN" }`, nothing is recorded (the credit and its per-IP counter are one transaction), and the next mint after unfreeze grants it once. `GET /api/v1/subscriptions/session-status` is refused while frozen with a 503 `EMERGENCY_FROZEN`, and its subscription upsert and initial credit are one transaction, so a freeze landing during the Stripe read leaves no partial subscription row and the replay credits once. Pre-existing: Stripe `invoice.paid` is now keyed by `billing_reason` — the first invoice (`subscription_create`) credits the same period as the checkout under the shared `sub:<id>:initial` key, `subscription_cycle` credits each later period once, and any other reason (a `subscription_update` proration, `manual`, …) credits nothing; a new subscriber's first month was previously credited twice when the first invoice was processed after the checkout. The freeze harness also lands the freeze inside the settlement forward's signing await, covering both forward-lifecycle send claims.
