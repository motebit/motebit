P0 containment: guardian recovery is suspended on this relay. A guardian-recovery key succession (`recovery` set, authorized by `guardian_signature`) is refused with 503 `GUARDIAN_RECOVERY_SUSPENDED` at `POST /api/v1/agents/:motebitId/rotate-key` and through the `succession` branch of `POST /api/v1/agents/register`, right after the body is read and before any guardian, key or identity read or write; the refusal is recorded in `relay_auth_events` as `succession:guardian_recovery_suspended`. `applySuccession`, the one writer of a recorded link, refuses a recovery record too, as a backstop for any later door. On main a guardian recovery re-keyed an identity on the proven guardian's signature with no caller check, and guardian provenance ignored the key the guardian was set under, so a current-key holder could plant a guardian that survived the owner's rotation (a persistent backdoor), and a stale client could revive a replaced guardian. Ordinary rotation (`old_key_signature`) and guardian registration are unchanged. The switch is `GUARDIAN_RECOVERY_ENABLED` in `guardian-recovery-containment.ts`, to be flipped back only with the permanent provenance fix.
