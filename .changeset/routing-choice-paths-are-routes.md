Documents the exact meaning of `routing_choice.routing_paths` and `alternatives_considered` on `DelegatedStepResult` and the execution-ledger step summary: `routing_paths[0]` is the PLANNED EXECUTION route — the hops the task takes from the caller to the worker (`[worker]` for a direct hire, `[peer_relay, worker]` for a federated one) — whose composed execution metrics `sub_scores` reflects; further entries are the non-dominated planned-route alternatives the policy weighed, best first. Adds the optional `trust_evidence_path?: string[]` — the vouching chain through every edge (recorded delegations included) behind `sub_scores.trust`, which may differ from the planned execution route. Every entry is a path that exists; a per-dimension optimum no single path attains is never reported. Neither path is a record of who executed the task (that is what receipts prove). Pinned hires report `[[worker]]` with `alternatives_considered: 0`. Types are unchanged; this pins the semantics that `motebit/execution-ledger@1.0` §4.1.1 and `motebit/delegation@1.0` §3.2 now state, matching the path-preserving ranking shipped in the reference relay.
