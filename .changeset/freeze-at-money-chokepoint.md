The emergency freeze now holds where money moves, not only at request entry. Allocation money's one writer, `moveAllocationMoney`, refuses first while frozen (`assertNotFrozen`, reading the PERSISTED freeze row inside the caller's transaction), and the forward lifecycle's new per-send claim `beginForwardSend` refuses the same way before EVERY `/federation/v1/settlement/forward` POST — the first send and each retry — so a freeze landing during one send stops every later one, and an already delivered or failed forward is never re-sent. `installFreezeMoneyGuards` (`services/relay/src/freeze.ts`) is the second layer: SQLite TEMP `BEFORE INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE` triggers on every money table (`relay_settlements`, `relay_federation_settlements`, `relay_transactions`, `relay_accounts`, `relay_x402_settlements`, `relay_withdrawals`, `relay_pending_withdrawals`, `relay_deposit_detector`) refuse every other money writer and any raw write. The settlement guards are column-narrowed — anchoring's `anchor_batch_id`, the P2P verification record and a forward's `pending → delivered` acknowledgement commit while frozen — and each anchoring cut (signed batch + leaf assignment) and each horizon cert + truncation is one transaction, so a freeze can no longer leave an orphan signed batch whose leaves a second batch anchors again. Forward-retry exhaustion never fails a retry whose refund the freeze would refuse; a claimed-but-unsettled task entry is never swept while frozen or for the claim hold after unfreeze. Every refusal is a typed 503 `EMERGENCY_FROZEN`, never a 500 or a 200: a withdrawal whose payout claim the freeze refused after its debit stays `pending` with its amount held; an x402 payment settled onchain whose credit was refused names its record (do not pay again); a round-2 dispute verdict whose fund action was refused is kept and finalized by the first read after unfreeze. Claims and queue entries are left as they were, so the work resumes once after unfreeze. Unfrozen behavior is unchanged. Harness: `freeze-money-chokepoint.test.ts` (request doors, background writers, every allocation-money kind with the triggers dropped; RED with either layer removed).
